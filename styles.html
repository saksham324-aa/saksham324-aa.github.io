Six styles, one editor

Each style has its own rules for type, colour, motion and sound, and each one can be recoloured for your brand.

Book an edit

Margin Notes

Everyday talking-head reels

Your face carries the reel while clean cards build on your words: a flow chart, a meter that fills, a number that counts up as you say it.

Yap

Reels with lines worth shouting

The talking head cuts to full-screen type on the key lines. Words land one by one, numbers fill meters, and every scene carries into the next.

Trailer

Episode launches and podcasts

Widescreen trailers cut to the beat of the music: a cold-open question, bass hits on the big words, and a cliffhanger cut to black.

Still Life

Voice notes, no camera needed

Send a voice note and every frame gets built: one photo-real object per sentence as a visual metaphor, with a new world every two seconds.

Live Edit

Explainers that must be watched

It looks designed live: gradient keywords, a cursor, counter cards and charts. About 70% motion graphics, 30% your face on the lines that matter.

Orbit

Podcast and stage clips

One seated shot becomes a scene. The guest is cut out, big keywords sit behind the head, and cards and a hologram ring orbit the body.
